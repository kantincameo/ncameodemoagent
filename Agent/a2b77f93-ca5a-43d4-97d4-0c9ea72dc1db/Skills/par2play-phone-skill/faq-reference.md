# PAR2PLAY Customer FAQ Reference

Use this Q&A reference to answer common customer inquiries. Answer in natural spoken language suitable for phone TTS.

## 👶 Junior Programs

**Q: Do you have programs for kids?**
A: Yes, we do! We offer Junior Golf Membership and summer camps.

**Q: What ages are the junior programs for?**
A: Our summer camp is for grades 3 through 6, and our Junior Membership program is age-grouped so kids of different ages can participate at appropriate levels.

**Q: How much is the junior program?**
A: Junior Membership is $249 per month or $399 per month depending on the tier. If you'd like more details on what's included, I can arrange for a callback from our team.

**Q: Who coaches the junior program?**
A: Our junior programs are coached by Nick and Sean, who are PGA-certified instructors.

## 🎉 Events & Parties

**Q: Can I host a private event or party?**
A: Absolutely! We can host private events in our climate-controlled bays with HDTVs and even karaoke capabilities.

**Q: What types of events do you host?**
A: We host birthday parties, corporate events, holiday parties, and leagues. Whatever type of event you're thinking of, we can likely accommodate it.

**Q: How do I book an event?**
A: You can reach out to info@par2play.com or I can arrange for one of our team members to give you a callback to discuss your event details.

**Q: Do you have food and drinks?**
A: We don't have a restaurant in-house, but we're tied up with local restaurants to provide an end-to-end experience, so we can arrange food and drinks for your event.
